Privacy

Last updated: November 2026. GOATlist is a small, free, fan-made voting site run by one person in Ontario, Canada. This page explains, in plain words, what we keep and why.

What we collect

  • Your votes. Each time you pick between two options we store which one you chose, tied to a random guest ID created in your browser. Your individual votes are never shown to anyone else. Only combined totals are public.
  • Your progress. Your level, streak, badges and the contenders you picked, so the site remembers them.
  • Your email, only if you give it. If you choose "Save with email", we store your email so you can sign in on other devices. We never sell it or send marketing.
  • Suggestions you send (a name and a list).
  • Simple usage counts, like "a list was started" or "a top 10 was finished", with your guest ID and a time. No cookies for tracking, no ads, no third-party trackers.

Where it's stored

Data is stored with Supabase (database and sign-in) and the site is hosted by Netlify. Your browser also keeps a small amount of data so you stay signed in.

What we don't do

We don't sell or share personal information, show ads, or use your data for anything except running GOATlist.

Your choices

You can stop using the site at any time. To delete your votes and account, or to ask what we hold about you, contact the site owner and we'll handle it within 30 days, as Canada's privacy law (PIPEDA) requires.

Age

GOATlist is for people 13 and older.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top.
